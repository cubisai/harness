# Nordic Calm

## World
A tool people keep open all day. Cool fog greys, plenty of air, one pine green for action. Calm, competent, never shouting; personality lives in small details (a warm accent dot, careful copy).

## Colour: restrained
- `--color-bg` fog, `--color-surface` white panels, `--color-surface-2` wells and hovers.
- `--color-primary` pine for the single primary action and selected states.
- `--color-accent` warm amber, sparingly: a new badge, an unread dot, one highlight.

## Type
- Onest for everything (`--font-sans`, `--font-display`). 16/24 body, 14/20 secondary, titles at 24–32 weight 600.
- Headings in sentence case; labels never all caps.
- `--font-mono` for IDs, code and keyboard shortcuts.

## Layout
- App shell: 240px sidebar or a 64px top bar; content max `--content-max`.
- Group with spacing and `--color-surface` panels rather than borders on everything.
- Tables with 48px rows and right-aligned numbers.

## Components
- Buttons: `--radius-md`, 40px tall (44 on touch); primary pine fill, secondary white with border, ghost text.
- Inputs: white, 1px border, focus ring 2px pine with 2px offset.
- Empty states: one line of plain guidance and a single action.

## Signature
A detail that rewards attention: a soft progress bar, a keyboard hint in the corner, a friendly empty state with a small line illustration in pine.

## Refuses
Gradients, heavy shadows, many accent colours, decorative hero metrics, marketing copy inside the app.

## Motion
Short (120–220ms) ease-out on panels and menus; nothing moves on its own.
