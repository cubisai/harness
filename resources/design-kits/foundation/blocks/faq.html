Questions, answered

Still stuck? Write to the team.

How do swaps work?

Staff offer a shift, a manager approves it, and the rota updates for both people at once.

Can I copy last week's rota?

Yes. Open any published week and choose Repeat week; every shift moves forward seven days.

Does it work for more than one site?

Each location keeps its own rota, and managers see every site they look after in one list.

What happens to hours at month end?

Worked hours add up as shifts close, so the export for payroll is ready on the last day.
