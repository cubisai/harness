# Deck patterns (16:9 slides, 1920×1080)

## Composition
- One idea per slide. The title is a full-sentence takeaway ("Subscribers grew 38% after we cut the price"), placed identically on every slide (same baseline, size, colour). 64–96px outer margins on a 12-column grid.
- At most three elements: title, one visual, one supporting line; leave ≥ 20% empty. ≤ 6 lines, ≤ 8 words per line; longer lists become a diagram or more slides.
- Rhythm across the deck: a cover, section dividers (full-bleed colour or photo with one big word), content slides, one big-number slide, a closing slide with the ask.
- Footer 12–14px: slide number and source, same place every time.

## Type and colour
Titles 48–72px (cover up to 120–160px), body 24–32px, nothing under 18px except the footer. Two typefaces, two colours plus neutrals for the whole deck.

## Anatomies
- **Chart slide**: one chart cropped to the comparison the title claims, direct labels, the highlighted bar/line in the accent, everything else grey, source + date in the footer.
- **Big number**: the number at 200–280px, one line of context, the source.
- **Photo slide**: full-bleed image, a solid or gradient scrim where text sits, contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Team / timeline**: ≤ 5 items laid out on a line or grid with real names, dates, roles.

## Refuse → do instead
- Topic-label titles ("Market") → takeaway sentences.
- Bullet walls and card grids → one visual per slide.
- Logos and decorative rules on every slide, new accents per slide → one consistent frame.
