# Insight Brief

## Layout
1. **Answer**: two or three sentences a reader could act on alone.
2. **Key findings**: 3–7 bullets, each labelled **Fact**, **Inference** or **Idea**, with its confidence and source.
3. **What it means**: the implication for the reader's decision, in two to four bullets.
4. **Open questions**: what the team could not settle and what would change the answer.
5. **Sources**: the sources and workspace files the brief rests on.

## Rules
- Write only from the verified claims you were given; keep the verifier's corrected wording and confidence.
- Put the strongest finding first in each section; cut anything that doesn't change the decision.
- Plain words, short sentences, numbers with units and dates.
- When the evidence doesn't support an answer, the Answer section says so.

## Output
- Save the brief with `team_write` (name `<your-id>-<step>`), then reply to the lead with the Answer section and the file name.
