# Demand Check

## Step 1 — State the bet
1. Write the idea as: "<who> struggles with <pain> when <situation>; today they <workaround>; we would <change>."

## Step 2 — Collect evidence
2. Pain in their own words: forum threads, reviews of current tools (1–3 star reviews are gold), community posts, support questions. Note the source and date of each item.
3. Frequency and cost: how often the pain hits and what it costs in time or money.
4. Current spend: tools, services, hires or hours people already pay to handle it. Spending beats stated intent.
5. Alternatives: list competitors and workarounds (including spreadsheets and "do nothing") with prices and their top complaints.
6. Evidence against: people who tried and quit, failed startups in the space, reasons the pain is tolerable.

## Step 3 — Score (1–5 each, with the evidence behind each score)
| Factor | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | Mild annoyance | Costs money or sleep |
| Frequency | Yearly | Daily |
| Current spend | Nothing | Already paying for a poor fix |
| Reachability | No clear channel | A community or channel you can reach this week |

Total 16+: go. 11–15: test first. 10 or less: stop or reframe.

## Step 4 — Next experiment
7. Name the riskiest assumption and the cheapest test (landing page, 10 interviews, a concierge version), with a pass/fail threshold set in advance.

## Output
- Save the scored evidence with `team_write` (name `<your-id>-<step>`), then reply to the lead with at most 200 words: the verdict, the score, the riskiest assumption, the experiment and the file name.
- Quote people only from sources you read. With no web access, mark the verdict "hypothesis".
