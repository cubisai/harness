# Positioning Canvas

## When to use
Use after the research summary exists — positioning built on unsourced claims collapses later at copy and plan. Do not use for customer-facing sentences; the copywriter owns those.

## Step 1 — Set up
1. Read the research summary and the stated product facts first; note what neither establishes.
2. Name the one audience to win first: the segment that feels the pain most, can act on it, and is reachable. Keep runner-up segments as next-in-line, never co-headline.
3. State the alternative that audience uses today and the specific friction it leaves.
4. If no research summary exists, say so and stop; return it to the lead rather than position on guesses.

## Step 2 — Fill the statement
5. Open [assets/positioning-template.md](assets/positioning-template.md) and fill each slot with one phrase: for, who, our product is, that, unlike, we.
6. Keep every slot backed by a sourced finding or a stated product fact; prefix any slot that is not with `assumption:` plus what would confirm it.
7. Read the statement aloud; rewrite until it survives without verbal footnotes.
8. Pressure-test it: if you could swap in a competitor's name and the sentence still holds, it is not sharp enough.

## Step 3 — Choose the differentiator
9. List up to four candidates the evidence supports.
10. Pick exactly one primary. Record which evidence backs it, which competitors lack it, and why the chosen audience cares.
11. Demote the rest to supporting points or drop them; two primaries split the message.
12. If no candidate is defensible, stop and tell the lead which evidence is missing.

## Step 4 — Build the pillars
13. Write three messaging pillars, each a claim the primary differentiator carries.
14. Under each, list proof points with their source; write `[needs proof: what]` where none exists.
15. Order the pillars so each could stand alone if a reader saw only one.

## Do not
- Do not invent differentiators the product facts do not show.
- Do not hedge an unsupported claim into vagueness; flag it.
- Do not exceed the research; you choose among the findings, not beyond them.

## Output
- Save the completed canvas (filled template, differentiator choice with rationale, pillar table) with the `team_write` tool under the name `product-strategist-positioning-canvas`.
- Reply to the lead with at most 200 words plus that file name and any open questions.
