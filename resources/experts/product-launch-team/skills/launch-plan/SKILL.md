# Launch Plan

## When to use
Use once the lead confirms the positioning and the launch window. Do not use for writing the assets themselves; the plan schedules them and their owners.

## Step 1 — Set the frame
1. State the goal as one measurable outcome with a number and a date.
2. Name the audience and the two or three places they already spend attention; those are the only channel candidates.
3. Keep at most three channels; cut the rest with a one-line reason each.
4. If the window is not four weeks, scale the weeks but keep the shape: build, warm up, launch, follow through.

## Step 2 — Fill the weeks
5. Open [assets/plan-template.md](assets/plan-template.md) and fill the four weeks: build and stage, warm up, launch, follow through.
6. Every row carries week, channel, owner role, asset needed, due date, metric and target.
7. Due dates sit before the week the asset runs in, never inside it.
8. Assign owners as roles, never person names; the team may not have the people yet.
9. Cap each week at 2–4 rows; a plan heavier than the team's proven capacity is noise, not ambition.

## Step 3 — Checklist and metrics
10. Draft the launch-day checklist: 8–12 ordered steps, each with an owner role and a time of day, so the day runs from a list, not from memory.
11. Split the metrics: leading ones the team moves this week (signups, replies, demo bookings) and lagging ones that arrive later (revenue, retention).
12. Give each metric a target value or `[target needed]`, and say they are placeholders until measured.
13. Name the threshold that means double down on a channel and the one that means stop or change it.

## Step 4 — Mark the cuts
14. List what to cut first when time runs short: lowest-reach channel work first, launch-day essentials last.
15. For each cut, note which metric it protects and what the plan keeps uncut.

## Do not
- Do not schedule a channel the audience does not already visit; reach beats novelty.
- Do not present budget, reach or rate guesses as facts; mark them estimates.
- Do not add a step that does not move the stated goal or the primary message.

## Output
- Save the full result (filled plan table, launch-day checklist, metric split with thresholds, cut list) with the `team_write` tool under the name `growth-planner-launch-plan`.
- Reply to the lead with at most 200 words plus that file name and any open questions.
